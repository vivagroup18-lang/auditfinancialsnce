1. Introduction & Scope

Welcome to the CA Financial Statement Automation Agent ("AuditFinancials", "we", "our", or "the Platform"), accessible at https://fintaxaudit.vivaproject.in. This platform provides specialized financial statement compilation, statutory reporting tools, and ICAI Non-Corporate Entity (NCE) Note Schedules for Chartered Accountants, tax auditors, accounting practitioners, and authorized client representatives.

This Privacy Policy explains how our application collects, uses, handles, stores, and protects personal and professional data when you use our service, including our integrations with Google Identity Services, Google Drive API, and Gmail API.

2. Google API Services & Limited Use Disclosure

Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

CA Financial Statement Automation Agent's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Our application accesses Google User Data exclusively to provide user-requested features within the CA Audit Workspace. We strictly adhere to the following limitations:

  • No External Transfer: We do not transfer Google user data to third parties, advertising networks, data brokers, or marketing platforms.
  • No Advertising: We never use or transfer Google user data for serving advertisements, including personalized, re-targeted, or interest-based advertising.
  • No AI Model Training: We do not use Google user data or financial records to train, retrain, or fine-tune generalized machine learning (ML) or artificial intelligence (AI) foundation models.
  • Human Review Limits: Human personnel do not read your Google Drive files or private accounting data unless you have given explicit consent for troubleshooting, it is necessary for security reasons, or required by applicable law.

4. Data Storage, Processing & Security Architecture

🔒 Local-First Architecture: Your financial data (trial balances, partner capital schedules, balance sheets, and notes) is processed locally inside your web browser. We do not store raw accounting transactions on our company servers.

  • In-Memory Access Tokens: OAuth 2.0 access tokens received from Google are stored in ephemeral browser session storage only. They are automatically cleared when you sign out or close your browser tab.
  • User-Owned Cloud Storage: When Google Drive Auto-Sync is activated, your backup files are stored directly in your own Google Drive storage under the folder CA Financial Statement Backups. You maintain full ownership and access control over these files.
  • Encryption: All data transmitted between your browser, our application, and Google APIs is encrypted using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).

5. Information Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

We operate with a strict confidentiality policy. We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal information or client financial records.

Information is shared only under the following limited conditions:

  • Cloud Infrastructure Providers: Google Cloud Platform and Firebase (used exclusively for hosting the web application, secure authentication, and cloud infrastructure).
  • Legal Requirements: If compelled by valid legal processes, statutory regulations, or court orders in accordance with applicable Indian laws.

6. User Controls, Access Revocation & Data Deletion

You have complete control over your data at all times:

  • Revoke Google Access: You can disconnect the application from your Google Account at any time by visiting Google Account Security Permissions.
  • Delete Drive Backups: You can directly delete the CA Financial Statement Backups folder or any backup file inside your Google Drive at any time.
  • Clear Local Browser Data: Use the "Clear Workspace" button or clear your browser's local storage to delete all locally cached company records immediately.
  • Account Deletion: To request complete deletion of any associated user profile records, contact our privacy contact below.
